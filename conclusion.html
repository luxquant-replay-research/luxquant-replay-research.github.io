Can this be profitable? straight up

Not with anything on this table, my guy. Four exit rules, four red numbers. That’s the whole tea. The best one still bleeds -93.36R.

The only universe where +3,243.62R exists is: you exit exactly at the claimed target, pay zero fees, and every target fills clean. That’s a spreadsheet flex, not a fill. The second you add a real stop and real fees, the bag evaporates.

For this to lowkey work, one of these would need to be true — and none of them are proven here:

  • Targets actually hold instead of reversing — they don’t; 94% bounced back.
  • You consistently get the claimed exit — nobody has shown fills at those prices.
  • A filter cuts the losers before entry — that’s a whole other research project, and we didn’t find one in this data.

Could some config out there work? Maybe. But we haven’t seen it, and we’re not gonna gaslight you into thinking this one does. No cap. And real talk: a past touch predicts nothing, so don’t go ape on leverage off a headline R.

For the admin real one, no shade

  • Label it straight: “target touched” is not “closed at target”. Two different animals.
  • Show the reversals. “TP hit, then SL” is the stat that matters, and it’s missing from most track records.
  • Include fees and funding. Gross R is cap; net R is the real deal.
  • Show the red months too. A track record with only green months is sus.
  • Let an outsider audit the ledger. That’s how trust gets built, fr.
Bottom line: the signals can hit targets and still not pay a follower. Until the track record shows closed fills with fees, treat claim R as vibes — not bread.

Receipts run the data yourself

Every number here is sliced from one snapshot and replayed on public Binance USD-M 1-minute candles. Open a window, click any signal, and check the charts plus the event timeline.

Claim = the exit implied by the provider’s published status (exit at the touched target, or −1R if reported loss). Real Replay Market = the same signal replayed on Binance USD-M 1-minute candles with the published entry, stop1, and an explicit exit rule per scenario.

Costs. Replayed R includes Binance USD-M maker 0.02% entry and taker 0.05% exit fees; funding is not applied. Claim R is a gross price ratio with no costs.

Limits. Intrabar order is unknown; candles that touch both the stop and a target are flagged, not guessed. Symbols not listed on Binance USD-M are excluded and shown honestly. This is independent research, not trading advice, and a past touch is not evidence of a profitable trade.
